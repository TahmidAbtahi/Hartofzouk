Welcome Home

Built on Connection,
Carried by Rhythm

Hart of Zouk began with a simple belief: that the DMV deserved a dance space that felt like home. Not just a venue — a community. Curated by @darlingdre, every gathering is designed with intention, warmth, and an open door.

"We don't prescribe a single path. We hold the floor so you can find yours."

From weekly Urbankiz Mondays in Falls Church to festival after-parties and intimate date-night workshops, we've hosted over 100 events across Washington DC, Northern Virginia, and Maryland — and we're just getting started.

Alongside the dancing, we offer studio rental and full event decor & design through our sister studio, Open Window Events.

Our Movements

Brazilian Zouk

Flowing, wave-like movement born in Brazil. Head movement, body isolation, pure expression.

Lambada

The rhythmic Bahian root of zouk — quick footwork, joyful energy, deep partner connection.

Kizomba

The Angolan foundation. Walking embrace, musical conversation, timeless intimacy.

Contemporary

Free-form modern dance — a language for the stories only movement can tell.

Ecstatic Dance

A shoeless, judgment-free floor for moving exactly how your body wants to.

Yoga

Breath, presence, and body — the practice that grounds everything we do.
