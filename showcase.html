The Evening

A Night of Performance & Connection

The Summer Showcase brings together artists from a variety of backgrounds to share original choreographies, explore movement, and inspire one another through performance.

Whether you're performing or cheering from the audience, this is a night to connect, support our community, and enjoy an exciting lineup of dance pieces.

We'll kick off with a Brazilian Zouk Social, pause for the showcase performances, and then continue dancing into the night.

Whether you're a dancer, friend, family member, or simply someone who loves the performing arts — we'd love to have you with us.
