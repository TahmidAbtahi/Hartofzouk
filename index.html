Welcome Home

A Space Built for Connection

We believe connection is not a luxury — it's a human need. Hart of Zouk is a collective where movement, music, and community come together, every single week.

This isn't about prescribing one style or one path. From Brazilian Zouk and lambada to urban kiz, tarraxo, and ecstatic dance, we offer a warm, supportive floor to grow your dance, deepen your connection, and build real relationships — with the music, with your partners, and with yourself.

Here is a place to slow down, connect, express — and remember why you fell in love with dancing.
